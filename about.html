About KJ Packs

Corrugated Packaging Manufacturing for Business Requirements

KJ Packs is based in Irugur, Coimbatore, serving industrial and bulk packaging requirements across Tamil Nadu and Kerala.

Experience-led manufacturing

The founder brings more than 30 years of industry experience. KJ Packs focuses on corrugated packaging solutions where dimensions, board construction, printing and quantity are discussed according to the buyer’s application.

Our stated monthly production capacity is 50+ tonnes, with construction options from 3-ply to 11+ ply.

We assess each enquiry based on box size and specifications; minimum order quantity is not fixed across all products.
