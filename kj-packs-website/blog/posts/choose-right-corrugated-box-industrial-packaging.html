How to Choose the Right Corrugated Box for Industrial Packaging

Key questions to consider when specifying corrugated cartons for storage, handling and dispatch.

Choosing a corrugated box is not only about selecting a length, breadth and height. Industrial packaging should be considered in relation to the product being packed, its weight, the way it is handled and the distribution conditions it may encounter.

1. Confirm the product dimensions

Measure the item or group of items to be packed. Decide whether the required box dimensions should be specified as inner dimensions or outer dimensions. This distinction matters because board thickness affects the usable internal space.

2. Consider the product and handling requirements

Think about the product's weight, shape, fragility, stacking conditions and how the carton will be moved. The packaging specification should reflect the actual use case rather than relying on a generic box description.

3. Discuss board construction

Corrugated packaging is available in different ply constructions. The appropriate construction depends on the product, dimensions, handling and distribution requirements. Buyers can discuss 3-ply, 5-ply, 7-ply, 9-ply and higher constructions with the manufacturer.

4. Specify printing and identification

Decide whether the carton should be plain, single-colour printed or multi-colour printed. If artwork, handling marks or product information are required, prepare the details for discussion before production.

5. Share quantity and delivery location

Include the required quantity and delivery pincode when requesting a quotation. Minimum order quantity can depend on the box size and specifications.

Information to include in a quotation request

  • Box type and product application
  • Length, breadth and height
  • Inner or outer dimensions and unit
  • Preferred ply, GSM and BF if known
  • Printing requirement
  • Quantity and delivery pincode
